Visual standards for this portfolio

The flagship visual pass was implemented with AI assistance on October 22, 2026. Its aim is publication-quality communication grounded in the existing evidence. It makes no claim to another analyst's authorship or endorsement.

  1. State the basketball question before the method name.
  2. Print the population, period, units and comparison on the figure.
  3. Show uncertainty without treating sensitivity samples as independent replications.
  4. Use direct labels and accessible contrast; do not rely on color alone for the main comparisons.
  5. Keep court geometry proportional. The OKC map uses the original five-foot cells, no smoothing, a zero-centered symmetric scale and common total-attempt denominators.
  6. Separate frequencies, probabilities, percentage points and points per attempt. Avoid implying that a frequency map measures pressure, openness or efficiency.
  7. Keep numerical result tables unchanged during a visual-only rebuild, and verify their hashes.
  8. Supply reproducible plotting code, high-resolution PNGs and vector SVGs. Preserve previous artifacts in Git history.

Each flagship repository includes tools/feature_charts.py, tools/chart_style.py and tools/VISUALS.md. The accompanying decision briefs are proposed workflows; no actual film review is implied. Exact validation and remaining source-data limitations remain in each repository's validation notes.
